Why this matters

A symptom is the start of the conversation, not the answer

A service representative may need the case, appliance profile, recent events, source freshness, and product guidance before choosing the next useful validation step.

Today’s friction

  • Context switching. Case and appliance evidence can live in separate places.
  • Evidence ambiguity. Source time, freshness, and missing readings are easy to lose.
  • Premature interpretation. A symptom or service code can be mistaken for a confirmed diagnosis.

The design response

  • Keep the case and appliance view in the same workflow.
  • Preserve units, timestamps, freshness, missing values, and unknowns.
  • Return only the evidence requested and authorized.
  • Leave the final service decision with a qualified person.

One contract, three surfaces

Use the same evidence semantics where service work happens

The experience changes with the host; the authorization and evidence boundary do not.

01 / CASE

Dynamics 365

Open Asset 360 beside the service case while preserving the existing workspace and Service Toolkit.

02 / SERVICE

Protected evidence

03 / CONVERSATION

Copilot and Cowork

Resolve a case or appliance through a bounded request and render the interactive Asset 360 card.

Representative journey

From “not draining” to an evidence-backed next question

The solution narrows the next validation step without inventing a cause or prescribing a repair.

  1. Begin with the reported symptomA customer says a Cove dishwasher is not draining.
  2. Resolve the authorized applianceThe case or conversation identifies one assigned appliance or returns an explicit ambiguous state.
  3. Load only the permitted evidenceProfile mode omits diagnostics; connected mode adds approved observations and source freshness.
  4. Ask for history or assessment when neededDeeper evidence is not loaded silently.
  5. Keep unknowns visibleObservations and limited interpretation remain distinct from a root-cause claim.
  6. Make a human service decisionA qualified person chooses the next validation, Customer Care, or certified-service step.

Customer-owned proof

Keep the first connected scope deliberately small

Replace every demo identity, source, mapping, and environment with approved customer-owned equivalents.

Proposed proof shape

One or two approved appliance models, two or three service conversations, one authorized read-only sample, explicit missing-data behavior, and no action from inside the evidence experience.

Decisions to agree

  • Identity, access groups, consent, and evidence roles.
  • Source systems, fields, units, timestamps, and freshness.
  • Case-to-customer-to-appliance relationships.
  • Customer Azure, Dynamics 365, Microsoft 365, monitoring, support, and rollback ownership.
  • Security, privacy, retention, regional, and network requirements.
